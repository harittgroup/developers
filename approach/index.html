Working together

What a project looks like.

1. Conversation

A short call or email thread to understand your goals, audience and constraints.

2. Proposal

A clear scope, timeline and price, with what's included and what isn't.

3. Build & launch

Regular check-ins, working previews and a careful launch.

4. Support & improve

Optional ongoing support, reporting and improvements based on real data.

Principles

How we hold ourselves accountable.

Honest expectations

Growth depends on your offer, market, budget and follow-through. We explain what we can influence and what we can't.

Real evidence only

We never invent testimonials, client logos or results. Anything we report is backed by data.

Purposeful AI

We recommend AI when it clearly helps, and say so when a simpler solution is better.

Built to last

Responsive, accessible, secure and maintainable, so you're not locked into us.

Privacy first

Analytics and forms follow consent and data-protection best practice.

Clear ownership

You own your domain, content, code and accounts. We document what we set up.
